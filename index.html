Support

Budget Duo helps couples keep track of shared bills, split them fairly and see who owes whom.

How do we share the budget?

One of you goes to Settings → Partner sync → Start sharing and sends the invitation. Your partner opens the link on their iPhone with Budget Duo installed. Both of you need to be signed in to iCloud.

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone. If you share your budget, it's stored end-to-end encrypted in the iCloud account of whoever started sharing. The developer has no servers and can't see your data.

How do I back up?

Settings → Backup → Export backup saves everything to a file. Restore from backup brings it back, also on a new phone.
